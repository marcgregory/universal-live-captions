HOW IT WORKS

From sound to on-screen captions in four steps

UniversalCaptions listens to whatever is playing on your PC ? no microphone, no extra cables. Gemini Live turns that audio into captions and translation in real time.

1

Capture system audio

UniversalCaptions listens to your PC's audio via WASAPI loopback ? the same audio you hear, in any app. No VB-CABLE, no microphone, no extra drivers.

2

Gemini Live transcription

The audio streams to a Gemini Live session that transcribes what's being said in real time ? no models to download, nothing to configure.

3

Translate to Tagalog

The same Gemini session translates English captions to natural Tagalog while the speaker is still talking.

4

Always-on-top captions

Captions stream onto a translucent, always-on-top overlay you can move, resize, and fade to fit any screen.

WHY UNIVERSALCAPTIONS

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it need a microphone?

No. UniversalCaptions uses WASAPI loopback to capture the audio your PC is already playing ? the same audio you hear. No microphone is used, and nothing is recorded.

Is my audio sent anywhere?

Audio is streamed to Google’s Gemini API for transcription and translation while captions run. Nothing is recorded, nothing is stored, and your API key never leaves Windows Credential Manager.

Which languages are supported?

Gemini Live handles a wide range of languages for both recognition and translation ? English ? Tagalog is the tested default pairing.

Does it work with any app?

Yes ? movies, video calls, meetings, podcasts, games, anything that plays sound on your PC. If Windows is playing audio, UniversalCaptions can caption it.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes ? captions are powered by Google's Gemini Live API, so an internet connection and a free Gemini API key are required. In exchange there is nothing to download or configure: no models, no Python, no setup.

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free forever, with no telemetry. You bring your own Gemini API key (Google offers a free tier).
